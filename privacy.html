Privacy Policy for 9571 Ledger

Effective October 4, 2026 · Applies to the 9571 Ledger QuickBooks Online integration

Who we are

9571 Ledger is a private, internal integration operated by 9571-8623 Québec inc., Montréal, Québec. It connects only to QuickBooks Online companies owned by the company and its owner. It is not offered to the public or to other QuickBooks users.

What the integration accesses

With the owner's authorization through Intuit's OAuth 2.0 sign-in, the integration reads and, when the owner approves a change, updates accounting data in the connected company: the chart of accounts, transactions, and financial reports such as the profit and loss statement, balance sheet and general ledger.

How the data is used

  • To prepare monthly financial statements and management reports for the company's owner and advisors.
  • To review and correct how transactions are categorized, with each change approved by the owner before it is applied.

The data is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with anyone outside the company except as described below.

Service providers

The integration runs on the owner's own computer. Report contents are processed by Anthropic's Claude AI assistant, which the owner uses to analyze the books, under Anthropic's commercial terms. Statements prepared from the data may be shared by the owner with the company's accountants and advisors.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored on the owner's computer in a file readable only by the owner's user account. Access can be revoked at any time from QuickBooks Online (Settings, Apps) or from the Intuit developer portal, which immediately stops all access.

Retention

Reports derived from the data are kept with the company's financial records for as long as Québec and Canadian record-keeping rules require. Tokens are deleted when access is revoked.

Your rights

Under Québec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector and Canada's PIPEDA, you may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask for corrections, or withdraw consent. The person responsible for personal information is Olivier Trudeau, reachable at oliviertrudeau@proton.me.
